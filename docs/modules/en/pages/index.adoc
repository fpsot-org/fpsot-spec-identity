= Secure a new service
:page-type: how-to
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This guide takes a team from a new public service to a sign-in and an {abbr-api-first} that meet this specification.
Each step cites the rule it meets.
The example is a service where a person files a request for a business.

== Choose the level

Assess the harm that a wrong identity could cause to the data, under IAM-001.
Cover its confidentiality, its integrity and its availability.
A request for a business that holds financial data fits the `substantial` level.
Check the mapping of your jurisdiction in `registry/assurance-levels.yaml`, under IAM-003.
In Québec, this service is at the level ID2 or ID3.
At ID3, codes sent by text message are not allowed, so plan for passkeys and authenticator apps.

== Set up the sign-in

Configure the {term-identity-provider} to require multifactor authentication and to offer passkeys, under IAM-010.
Let each person enroll a second authenticator, under IAM-013.
Register the backend of the front end as a confidential client with `private_key_jwt`, under IAM-025.
Put the issuer identifier alone in the `aud` of its client assertion.
Ask for the level in `acr_values`, and check `acr` in the identity token, under IAM-023.

== Build the backend for the front end

Keep the tokens on the server, under IAM-021.
Give the browser one cookie, marked `Secure` and `HttpOnly`, with `SameSite=Strict`.
Set the session limits for the public: 24 hours overall and 1 hour idle, under IAM-040.
Use RP-Initiated Logout and Back-Channel Logout, under IAM-042.

== Protect the API

Accept only tokens whose `aud` names your {abbr-api}, under IAM-030.
Publish the metadata of your {abbr-api} at `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource`, under IAM-033.
For an operation that needs the `high` level, answer as below when the token falls short, under IAM-034.

[source,http]
----
HTTP/1.1 401 Unauthorized
WWW-Authenticate: Bearer error="insufficient_user_authentication",
  acr_values="https://fpsot.org/assurance-levels/high"
Content-Type: application/problem+json

{
  "type": "https://fpsot.org/problems/identity/insufficient-user-authentication",
  "title": "A stronger or more recent sign-in is needed.",
  "status": 401,
  "acrValues": ["https://fpsot.org/assurance-levels/high"]
}
----

== Ask the authorization engine

On each request, send an evaluation to the {term-authorization-engine} over AuthZEN, under IAM-050.
Name the person and the request by opaque identifiers, under IAM-057.
Pass the business that the person acts for in the context, under IAM-064.

[source,json]
----
{
  "subject": {"type": "user", "id": "u-7f3c2a"},
  "action": {"name": "submit"},
  "resource": {"type": "request", "id": "r-1029"},
  "context": {"representedPartyId": "p-55e0"}
}
----

Deny when the answer is `false`, when the engine fails, or when it is late, under IAM-051.
Store the relationship "representative of this business" in the engine, under IAM-061.
Give it a scope, an end date and a reference to its proof.

== Record the events

Emit `org.fpsot.identity.access.evaluated` for each decision, under IAM-092.
Name the person and the business by their opaque identifiers only, under IAM-093.
The FPSOT observability specification sets how to keep these events.
