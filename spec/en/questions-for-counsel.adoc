= Questions for counsel
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This page is informative.
It sets no rule, and it is not legal advice.
It lists the legal questions that this specification raises but cannot answer.
A lawyer must answer each one before {abbr-fpsot-first} turns it into a rule.
An article number marked _to be verified_ comes from a secondary source, not from the law itself.

== Questions

. *Token exchange between organizations.* A token exchange can pass user context from one public body to another.
Does that disclose personal information, and which authority does it need under the Québec act, chapter A-2.1?
Which authority does it need under the federal Privacy Act?
Does it call for a privacy impact assessment?
The answer decides whether a later version can allow the exchange that IAM-065 forbids today.

. *Scope of the Québec rules.* Public bodies must follow the link:https://www.publicationsduquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fileadmin/gazette/pdf_encrypte/lois_reglements/2024F/83715.pdf[Québec rules on digital identity assurance].
Can {abbr-fpsot}, a Québec non-profit, count as a provider under contract under article 12 when it supplies a reference stack?
Article 23 bars an agreement from removing the liability of the public body.
Does that affect the warranty disclaimers of {abbr-fpsot} licenses?

. *Proof of capacity.* Articles 13 and 14 of the Québec rules require proof of the capacity to act.
Which evidence meets them for a tutor, a mandatary under a protection mandate and a representative of a business?
Can a relationship in the authorization engine that points to a stored document meet that need, under IAM-061?

. *Age.* link:https://www.quebec.ca/gouvernement/travailler-gouvernement/normes-gouvernance-pratiques-internes/protection-des-renseignements-personnels/consentement/personnes-autorisees-a-consentir[Québec guidance on consent] lets a minor aged 14 or over consent to the use of their information.
The Québec shared sign-in service also accepts people from age 14.
Does a sign-in at that age fit the rules of the Civil Code of Québec on the capacity of minors?

. *Biometrics in passkeys.* A passkey often unlocks with a fingerprint or a face, and the template never leaves the device.
Does that create a database of biometric characteristics?
If so, must the public body declare it to the Commission d'accès à l'information 60 days before use?
Law firms cite article 45 of chapter C-1.1 for that duty, _to be verified_.
Does the passkey need express consent under article 44 of the same act, _to be verified_?

. *Personal information in audit records.* The events of IAM-091 hold `sub`, a session identifier and resource identifiers.
Do they count as personal information?
Which rules on keeping and destroying them apply, under article 28 of the Québec rules and other Québec laws?

. *Language of sign-in pages.* {abbr-fpsot} software can show sign-in pages and errors to the public for a Québec public body.
Does the Charter of the French language, as amended in 2022, set rules for them?
Do federal rules on official languages impose rules on federal adopters?

. *Licenses of cited texts.* May {abbr-fpsot} quote text from GovStack and from the architecture of the European digital identity wallet?
May it quote OpenID Final specifications and IETF RFCs?
Is a mere reference to the OWASP ASVS free of the share-alike duty of its license?

. *Steward under the Cyber Resilience Act.* Public bodies in the European Union may adopt the reference stack.
Would {abbr-fpsot} then be an open-source software steward for the components it publishes?

. *Naming a product with one main maintainer.* The page xref:reference-stack.adoc[Reference stack] names the reference authorization engine.
Does that create a duty of care, or a risk under the rules on fair procurement?
Does a named fallback reduce that risk?
