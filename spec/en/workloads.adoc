= Machine and workload identity
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

Services call other services, and each call needs to prove which software is calling.
These rules give each workload its own identity and keep the identity of a person from leaking between services.
The link:https://www.publicationsduquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fileadmin/gazette/pdf_encrypte/lois_reglements/2024F/83715.pdf[Québec rules on digital identity assurance] also apply to machine identities, in article 29.

== Identity of a workload

[[IAM-070]]
*IAM-070.* Each workload MUST have its own cryptographic identity.
The identity MAY be a link:https://spiffe.io/docs/latest/spiffe-about/overview/[SPIFFE] identifier with its certificate or token.
It MAY instead be a client that the authorization server registers with its own key.
Two workloads MUST NOT share an identity, and the test and production copies of one workload MUST have different identities.
Each workload MUST receive only the audiences and scopes that it needs.
A distinct identity per workload lets an operator revoke one part without stopping the others.

[[IAM-071]]
*IAM-071.* A call between two services MUST use mutual {abbr-tls-first}, or a token bound to the key of the caller.
A workload that gets its own token MUST use the client credentials grant with `private_key_jwt` or mutual {abbr-tls}, under IAM-025.
A call inside one network still crosses a trust boundary, since an attacker inside can reach every service.

[[IAM-072]]
*IAM-072.* A service MUST NOT pass the {term-access-token} of a person on to another {abbr-api-first}.
When the next service needs to know the person, the first service MUST exchange the token under IAM-065.
A token sent onward reaches an audience it was not made for, and that audience can then replay it.

[[IAM-073]]
*IAM-073.* A call between two organizations MUST authorize the calling organization and its system, not a person.
The receiving organization MUST check the identity of the calling system under IAM-070.
A written agreement between the two organizations MUST set what the calling system may ask for.
This follows the model of link:https://specs.govstack.global/overview/25q3/security-requirements/7-auth-services[GovStack], which passes no user token between organizations.
Each organization stays responsible for what its own staff do.

[[IAM-074]]
*IAM-074.* Each key and certificate of a workload MUST have an owner and an expiry, and MUST rotate under SEC-023.
A certificate or token of a workload SHOULD last 24 hours or less, with automatic renewal.
The private key MUST stay in a secret store or in hardware, under SEC-020.
The organization MUST keep an inventory of the identities of its workloads, with their owners.
A short life and an inventory turn a leaked key into a contained incident.
