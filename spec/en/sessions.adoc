= Sessions and sign-out
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A session lets a person act for a while after one sign-in.
These rules set how long a session lasts, when it ends and how sign-out reaches every application.

== Time limits

[[IAM-040]]
*IAM-040.* A session MUST end at the time limits of this table, or sooner.
The overall limit counts from the sign-in, and the idle limit from the last action of the person.
A staff session follows the federal link:https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/guideline-multi-factor-authentication.html[guideline on multifactor authentication].
A session of the public follows link:https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html[NIST SP 800-63B-4].
An organization MAY set shorter limits.

[cols="2,1,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Who |Overall, `low` and `substantial` |Idle, `low` and `substantial` |Overall, `high` |Idle, `high`
|Staff |12 hours |30 minutes |12 hours |15 minutes
|Public |24 hours |1 hour |12 hours |15 minutes
|===

A person at a public counter or on a shared computer needs the shorter limits of staff.
Longer limits for the public spare people from signing in again on their own devices.

[[IAM-041]]
*IAM-041.* When a session ends, the person MUST sign in again at the level of the service.
The backend of a browser front end MUST then drop the tokens of the session.
The authorization server MUST revoke its refresh tokens.
The application MUST create a new session identifier at each sign-in and at each step up.
A session identifier MUST be random, with at least 128 bits of entropy.
A fixed identifier lets an attacker plant a session before the person signs in.

== Sign-out

[[IAM-042]]
*IAM-042.* An application MUST offer a sign-out that ends its own session and the session at the {term-identity-provider}.
It MUST use link:https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-rpinitiated-1_0.html[OpenID Connect RP-Initiated Logout 1.0] for that.
The identity provider MUST then tell every other application of the session, with link:https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-backchannel-1_0.html[OpenID Connect Back-Channel Logout 1.0].
Each application MUST end its session when it receives that notice.
An identity provider and its applications SHOULD also exchange session and credential events under link:https://openid.net/specs/openid-caep-1_0-final.html[OpenID Continuous Access Evaluation Profile 1.0].
A person who signs out on a shared computer expects every service to sign out.

[[IAM-043]]
*IAM-043.* A session MUST keep the assurance level of the service for its whole life.
When the level falls below the level of the service, the application MUST end the session.
It MAY instead require a step up before the next action.
The level falls when the person removes the authenticator of the sign-in, or when the account loses its verification.
The Québec rules require that a session end when its level falls below the required level.

[[IAM-044]]
*IAM-044.* A service SHOULD show each person a list of their active sessions.
The list SHOULD give the device and the time of each sign-in.
The person SHOULD be able to end any of them.
People often spot a stolen account first through a session they do not know.
