# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT identity and access specification.

The specification covers how public-sector software identifies and authenticates people and workloads, and how it decides their access: assurance levels and their mapping to the levels of governments and other frameworks, authenticators, passwords, account recovery and the response to a compromise, OpenID Connect and OAuth 2.0 clients and authorization servers, the checking of tokens at APIs, sessions and sign-out, authorization through a policy engine over a standard interface, the portability of policies and relationships, representation of another person or a business and delegation, workload and machine identity, provisioning, deprovisioning and federation of accounts, the identity and access audit events and their content, and the problem types of identity and access errors. It includes data files for the level mapping and the problem types, and an informative reference stack.

It does not cover how a system records, keeps and protects audit events, which the FPSOT observability specification covers, digital identity wallets and verifiable credentials, identity proofing procedures, or legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
